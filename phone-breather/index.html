How it works

The phone accelerometer reads 3D motion at device-native rate. A PCA calibration window finds the dominant breathing axis — the direction your belly pushes the phone forward on inhale. The projection onto that axis is normalised to a 0–1 volume using inverse lerp between calibration bounds, matching the polar breathing processor's derivative and phase classification approach.

Place the phone screen-up on your lower belly, lie back comfortably, and tap Start. Calibration takes about 10 seconds of normal breathing.

Experimental breath demo, not a medical device. Accelerometer-derived volume is a motion surrogate, not airflow or lung volume.
